About This System

🧠 What is Speech Emotion Recognition?

Speech Emotion Recognition (SER) is the process of automatically identifying a human's emotional state from their speech signal. The voice carries rich information beyond words — pitch, rhythm, energy, and tone all reflect emotional states like happiness, anger, sadness, and more.

📊 What is MFCC?

Mel Frequency Cepstral Coefficients (MFCCs) are the primary feature for speech processing. They represent how the human ear perceives different frequencies (logarithmic mel scale). MFCCs capture the vocal tract shape that differs between emotional states — making them perfect for SER.

⚠️ Limitations & Disclaimer

  • Emotion recognition from voice is probabilistic, not certain.
  • Background noise can significantly affect prediction accuracy.
  • The model was trained on RAVDESS (English, professional actors) — it may not generalize to all accents, languages, or speaking styles.
  • Short recordings (< 2 seconds) may yield less reliable predictions.
  • This system detects expressed speech emotion, not true psychological state.
  • Microphone quality affects audio quality and therefore prediction accuracy.
